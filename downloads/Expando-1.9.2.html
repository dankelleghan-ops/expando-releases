Expando 1.9.2 (19)

Fixed: in Mail, a letter typed right after an autocorrect could land in front of the corrected word ("ihow's"). Short autocorrect fixes are now typed in instead of pasted, so that can't happen, and they leave your clipboard alone. New since 1.7.1 (14): bold and italic in snippets (select words and click Bold or Italic, or press ⌘B or ⌘I); lines that start with "• " or "1. " paste as real bulleted or numbered lists in Mail, Gmail and Notes; pasting formatted text into a snippet, such as an email copied from Mail, keeps its bold, italics, links and lists; Undo and Redo buttons in the Snippet Editor; and a handle under the Content box that you drag to make it taller or shorter. Also fixed: after a snippet was pasted, the Mac could act as if the Command key were still held down.
