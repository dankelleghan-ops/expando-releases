Expando 1.7.1

Expando 1.7.1: a letter typed right after an autocorrect no longer lands in front of the corrected word. New since 1.4.0: Search Snippets (Control-Option-S), web addresses paste as clickable links (also from {clipboard}), your whole clipboard is kept safe during expansions, automatic daily backups with Restore from Backup, Check My Snippets, Import and Export, a menu-bar warning while a password field pauses Expando, and a shorter shortcut now waits for a longer one.
